## 🎯 The Problem We're Solving

Technical interview preparation is **broken**:

- 😞 Students practice alone with LeetCode, missing real interview dynamics
- 💸 Quality mock interviews are expensive and hard to find
- 🔀 Tab-juggling between Google Meet, code editors, and whiteboards
- 🤝 Getting referrals depends on who you know, not what you know
- ❌ No unified platform to track your preparation journey

## 💡 The Zepruv Solution

We're building a **complete ecosystem** that takes you from your first practice session to your final job offer.
